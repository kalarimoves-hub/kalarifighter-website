About Kalaripayattu

Kalaripayattu is an ancient Indian martial art from Kerala, with a history of over 3,000 years. It is a complete system that brings together physical training, combat techniques, flexibility, breath control and mental discipline.

Meet Your Instructor

My name is Krishna. I am a dedicated Kalaripayattu and Yoga teacher, trained in the traditional style from Kerala, India. I believe that in order to be peaceful, one must learn how to fight.

My teaching combines authentic Kalaripayattu techniques with a modern, safe approach, so it is open to people of all ages.
